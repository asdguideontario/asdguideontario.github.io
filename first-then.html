First–Then boards: first the task, then the good part

A First–Then board shows your child two pictures: the task to do first, and the fun thing that comes right after. It makes what happens next easy to see and understand. Make one here, use it live on a phone or tablet, or print and laminate it.

A large review of autism research (National Clearinghouse on Autism Evidence and Practice, 2020) lists visual supports — such as First–Then boards and picture schedules — as an evidence-based practice.

Your boards

Make a board for each routine — for example, one for homework and one for getting dressed. Tap a picture square to choose a picture.

Quick start:

Boards are saved on this device only.

How to use it well

  • Show the board before you start, and point as you say the words: “First shoes, then park.”
  • Keep the First task short and something your child can do.
  • Always give the Then right away — keeping the promise builds trust.
  • Use things your child really likes for Then, and let them choose when they can.
  • Use the same words every time. Later, you can move on to a longer picture schedule.

Use it with the other tools

Practise the skill with a social story, and track progress with a reward chart.
